Where we came from

Our story

it started on a tiny 60 cm griddle

Brasa Smash was born in a garage in Pontal, with a small griddle, a heavy spatula and half a dozen neighbors as its first customers. The idea was simple: a ball of fresh beef, pressed hard onto a very hot griddle until the edges turned lacy and crisp.

The neighborhood liked it, the line reached the sidewalk and the griddle got too small. Today the place is bigger, but the method is the same: everything made to order, with few ingredients and a griddle that’s always very hot. Walk down Rua Exemplo after six and you’ll smell it before you see the sign.
